3. Do not introduce external facts, examples, definitions, or assumptions.

- Retain the original technical terms.

- Ask questions that can be answered directly using the supplied notes.

- Provide answers to all 10 questions.
- Include a brief explanation for each answer.
- Identify the supporting statement or concept from the notes.
- Do not provide unsupported answers.

Before presenting the final study pack, verify:

1. Are all major topics covered in the summary?
2. Has the original terminology been preserved?
3. Are there exactly 10 questions?
4. Does every MCQ have exactly four options?
5. Is there exactly one correct answer per MCQ?
