## 1. SUMMARY

A computer network is a collection of interconnected devices that communicate and share resources. Networks consist of nodes and communication links. Protocols are sets of rules that govern how data is transmitted and received between devices. The OSI model organizes network communication into seven layers: Physical, Data Link, Network, Transport, Session, Presentation, and Application.

## 2. KEY CONCEPTS

- **Computer Network:** A collection of interconnected devices that communicate and share resources with one another.
- **Nodes and Communication Links:** Nodes are devices such as computers, servers, and printers. Communication links connect these devices and allow data to travel between them.
- **Protocol:** A set of rules that governs communication between devices in a network, specifically defining how data is transmitted and received.
- **OSI Model:** A framework that divides network communication into seven layers: Physical, Data Link, Network, Transport, Session, Presentation, and Application.
